The assistant (chat)

  • When you write in the chat, your messages are sent to our AI provider, Groq, only to generate the answer.
  • Hipnos AI does not save your conversation. If you reload the page, the chat starts again.
  • Please do not share sensitive personal data (ID numbers, health, banking details) in the chat.
  • AI answers may contain mistakes. For anything important, contact us directly.

Hosting and security

The website is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web server, it processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the site, protect it from abuse and limit the number of chat messages per visitor.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies for advertising or tracking.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we have about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to edimatus2016@gmail.com.
